Style guide

The design system for ismercuryretro.com. These are the tokens and components the live site already uses (tokens.css mirrors index.html), plus the logo.

Principle

The answer is the page. One word, set huge, in the color of its state: burnt orange for yes, teal for no. Everything else is quiet, narrow and legible: a single 460px column, soft panels, monospace for data.

Logo

The Mercury symbol ☿ in the page’s ink color next to ℞, the astrologers’ symbol for retrograde (an R with a crossed leg), in retrograde orange.

Icon sizes

The ☿ alone for avatars and in-page marks. White ℞ on an orange tile for favicon, home-screen and app icons. The bare glyph for small in-page marks.

Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space of half the logo height on every side (the dashed box). Smallest sizes: lockup 80px wide, ☿ 16px tall, tile 16px, bare ℞ 12px tall.

Rules

Use assets/logo.svg on light backgrounds and assets/logo-dark.svg on dark ones. Don’t recolor the glyphs, redraw or retype them, stack the lockup, or put it on a busy or low-contrast background. The bare ℞ is always retrograde orange (--yes) or white on orange.

Shape and space

One 460px column, 20px between blocks. Radius shrinks with importance: verdict 22, panel 16, stat 14, tab 12, control 10, tag 6, chip and button pill. Borders are 1px --line. No shadows. Every control is at least 44px tall.

Motion and access

No decorative animation. Buttons move 1px when pressed, and not at all with reduced motion. Focus shows a 2px outline in the state color. Answers are announced (role="status"), and state never relies on color alone: the word YES or NO always shows.

Voice

Answer first, then facts. Plain verbs, no horoscope language. Say what a control does: “Buy me a coffee”, “Today”.
