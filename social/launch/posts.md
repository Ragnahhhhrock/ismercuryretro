## LinkedIn (linkedin-1200x627.png)

I just launched ismercuryretro.com.

The idea is simple: ask "Is Mercury retrograde?" and get one word back. YES in orange, NO in teal, with a live countdown and the dates of the current or next retrograde.

I wanted to see how far a single-question product could go with a tight design system: one word set huge, one 460px column, mobile first, no clutter. Every extra element had to earn its place.

It's live now, just in time for the October 24 to November 13 retrograde.

Try it and tell me what you'd cut or add: ismercuryretro.com
